About Us

JND Serv & Construction
Supplies Trading
Davao City · Est. 2025

Supplying Davao's
builders & growers.

JND Serv and Construction Supplies Trading is a sole proprietorship based in Poblacion District, Davao City, dealing in general merchandise, construction supplies and materials, agricultural equipment, and a wide range of related trading services for individual customers and business clients.

Vision

To become a leading supplier recognized for quality products, reliable service, and long-term partnership that support our customers' growth and success.

Mission

To deliver high-quality products on time, at competitive prices, while maintaining strong relationships with customers and continuous improvement to meet evolving market needs.
